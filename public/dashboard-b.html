What the terms mean

How it is scored: Kicktipp points after 90 minutes: 4 exact score, 3 correct goal difference, 2 correct outcome. The last tip before kickoff counts; wrong and missing tips score 0. The odds favourite tips 2:1 for the home or 0:1 for the away favourite, the consensus the median of all model tips.
